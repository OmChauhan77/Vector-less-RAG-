thereof.

b) The CONTRACTOR shall have furnished the Initial Security Deposit as stipulated in
Clause 2.1.1.0 and associated clauses hereof.

c) The CONTRACTOR shall have executed the formal contract in terms of the Form of
Contract.

d) The CONTRACTOR shall have made a formal application for the release of the
Mobilization Advance and shall have furnished a Bank Guarantee to cover the

Scheduled Bank in India acceptable to the OWNER and in a format approved by the
OWNER.

percent) above the State Bank of India declared rate for cash credit advances prevailing

g) All recoveries shall first be apportioned and appropriated to interest and then to principal.
