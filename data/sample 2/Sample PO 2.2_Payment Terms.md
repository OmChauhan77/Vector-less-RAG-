## 1.0 MOBILIZATION ADVANCE

Interest bearing Mobilization Advance (Rate of interest @ 7.70% per annum) shall be paid as per the
General conditions of Contract in minimum two instalments against BG for 110% of the value of the
advance applicable GST.


## 2.0 SECURED ADVANCE ON MATERIALS

Secured Advance shall be paid as per the General Conditions of Contract. However, no Secured Advance
shall be paid against the material wherever Milestone Payment has been recommended on receipt and
acceptance of material at site.

Engineer-in-charge after affecting the necessary deductions/recovery, if any. The basis for payment against
